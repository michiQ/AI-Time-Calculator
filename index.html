How the math works (and what it cannot tell you)

Pivot time = one-month baseline + the illustrative time assigned to each selected driver. The model is additive and not empirically calibrated. Driver times can overlap in real organizations.

Annual friction = in-scope operating spend × the sum of assumed driver friction percentages. Late-pivot exposure = months beyond disruption × (spend × annual value-at-risk percentage ÷ 12).

Combined exposure adds one year of friction and incremental delayed-response exposure. It assumes those components do not overlap. Verify actual process data, costs, dependencies, and potential double counting before making investment decisions.

Money terms explained simply

A quick reference for reading the calculator and interpreting its estimates.

Revenue, operating expenses, and operating income

Revenue is the money a business earns from customers before costs.

Direct costs are the costs of delivering its products or services.

Operating expenses (OpEx) are the other costs of running the business, such as administration, IT, sales, and marketing. In some accounting presentations, the term is used more broadly, so avoid counting direct costs twice.

Operating income means operating profit, not another stream of money coming in. It is what remains after direct costs and other operating expenses, before interest and taxes.

Operating margin is operating income divided by revenue.

Simple example

Annual revenue$20,000,000
Less direct costs−$8,000,000
Less other operating expenses−$9,000,000
Operating income$3,000,000
Operating margin15%

If $900,000 of the existing expenses is avoidable organizational friction, that's equivalent to 30% of current operating income. Removing all of it could increase operating income to $3.9M, assuming nothing else changes. It is not a new $900,000 expense.

Calculator estimates are rough, assumption-based illustrations. A selected dropdown is not evidence of a specific dollar loss. Validate the rates with payroll, process time, duplicated software costs, and actual rework before using figures for financial decisions.
